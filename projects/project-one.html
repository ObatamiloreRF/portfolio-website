END-TO-END ANALYTICS

Restaurant Market Intelligence

Transforming Yelp business and review data into a practical decision-support tool for restaurant market-entry and investment decisions.

Python Pandas Statsmodels Tableau Data Modelling

THE CHALLENGE

How can restaurant data support better market-entry decisions?

Restaurant investors face high-impact decisions around where to open, what type of restaurant concept to pursue, and which operating characteristics may support stronger customer engagement.

This project created a structured analytical framework designed to complement traditional market research with data-driven insights across location, cuisine, competition, customer engagement and restaurant characteristics.

THE PROCESS

From raw data to decision tool.

01

Data Preparation

Assessed the Yelp business and review datasets, identified restaurant records, filtered the relevant markets and retained currently open restaurants.

02

Cleaning & Integration

Standardized restaurant attributes, preserved missing values where assumptions were not appropriate and integrated restaurant-level review information.

03

Feature Engineering

Created primary cuisine classifications, city-level benchmarks and city-cuisine measures to enable more meaningful market comparisons.

04

Predictive Modelling

Developed restaurant-level and city-cuisine-level regression models to understand factors associated with customer engagement.

05

Opportunity Analysis

Combined customer engagement and relative supply to identify city-cuisine combinations that could warrant further investigation.

06

Visualization

Translated the analytical outputs into an interactive Tableau dashboard designed for non-technical decision-makers.

MODELLING THE MARKET

Two analytical perspectives.

MODEL 01

Restaurant-Level Engagement

Modelled logged Yelp review count using observable restaurant characteristics such as city, cuisine, pricing and service attributes.

41.3% Training R²
39.0% Testing R²

MODEL 02

City × Cuisine Market Analysis

Shifted the analysis to city-cuisine combinations to support location and restaurant concept screening at a broader market level.

89.1% Training R²
84.4% Testing R²

Model performance was interpreted as decision-support evidence, not as a guarantee of future restaurant success.

THE FINAL PRODUCT

Turning analysis into an interactive decision tool.

The final Tableau dashboard allows users to explore four related questions: where to open, what restaurant concept to pursue, how operating characteristics relate to customer engagement, and where engagement appears attractive relative to competition.

KEY FINDINGS

What the analysis revealed.

01

Location matters

Reno, New Orleans and Santa Barbara showed strong adjusted predicted review activity, while Philadelphia provided the largest market scale.

02

Cuisine is market dependent

Cuisine performance varied across markets, supporting the need to evaluate location and restaurant concept together rather than independently.

03

Engagement and competition belong together

Comparing customer engagement with existing restaurant supply helped identify city-cuisine combinations that may deserve additional investigation.

ANALYTICAL JUDGEMENT

A decision-support tool, not a prediction of profitability.

Yelp review activity was used as a proxy for customer engagement, but it should not be interpreted as revenue, profitability or return on investment.

Factors such as rent, foot traffic, marketing, restaurant age, operating costs and site-specific competition were not fully captured in the available data.

The final tool is therefore best used to narrow down promising markets and restaurant concepts for further financial and market research.
